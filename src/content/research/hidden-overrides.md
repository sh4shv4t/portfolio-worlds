My B.Tech project, with Harshit Nayan and Shailesh Nehra, supervised by Dr. Subrat Dash. We build small model organisms with hidden triggers and then dig into what's going on inside (individual neurons, probes and so on) when the reasoning says one thing and the answer does another. Paper in progress.
