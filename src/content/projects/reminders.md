A tiny reminders app for Windows. It runs a small PowerShell server on localhost, opens in an Edge app window and pings your phone through [ntfy](https://ntfy.sh) when something's coming up.

## Fun bits

- A different theme every month
- No accounts, no cloud: your data stays in a folder on your machine
- Phone notifications without building a phone app
